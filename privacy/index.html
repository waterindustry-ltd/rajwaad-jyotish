Privacy Policy

Rajwaad Jyotish · Effective date: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Rajwaad Jyotish ("the App", "we", "us") collects, why, and how it is handled. We wrote this policy to match exactly what the App does — no more, no less.

1. Overview

Rajwaad Jyotish is an astrology, horoscope, and kundli (birth chart) app for entertainment purposes. To personalise your readings, the App asks you to enter your birth details during onboarding, and offers an AI chat feature that answers your astrology questions using those details. The App does not use email/password sign-in — instead it identifies your installation with an anonymous, device-generated recovery code you can save and use to restore your data on another device.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Birth details you enter

During onboarding, the App asks you for: name, gender, date of birth, time of birth, and place of birth. This information is used to calculate your kundli/horoscope and to personalise the readings and AI chat responses the App generates for you. It is stored on your device and is also sent, together with your questions, to the AI service described in section 2.2 so that service can generate a reading — see section 4 for how that data is shared.

2.2 AI chat (astrology questions)

When you use the App's AI chat feature, the questions you type and your birth details are sent over the internet to a third-party AI service operated by our technology provider (endpoint dev.bbltech.org, backed by a DeepSeek-based language model) so it can generate a personalised astrology response. Your chat history for a session may also be retrieved back from that server so it can be displayed to you. This feature requires an internet connection and does not function offline.

Readings generated by the AI chat are for entertainment purposes only — see the disclaimer in our Terms of Use.

2.3 Anonymous account (recovery code & device ID)

The App does not require you to create an account with an email or password. Instead, it generates a random recovery code tied to your installation, which you can save and later enter on another device to restore your birth details, chat history, and settings. The App also generates a random device identifier (UUID) used internally to associate your data with your installation. Neither the recovery code nor the device identifier is linked to your real-world identity by us.

2.4 Notifications

The App requests the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission to send you optional daily horoscope notifications, and uses RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED solely to reschedule those notifications after your device restarts. Declining notification permission does not affect any other feature of the App.

2.5 Network & vibration

The App requests INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE to connect to the AI chat service, load remote configuration, and show ads, and VIBRATE to give haptic feedback on certain interactions. The App does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, SMS, or any other sensitive device permission.

2.6 Analytics, diagnostics, and configuration (Firebase)

The App uses Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Analytics — collects app-interaction events and device/app information to help us understand how the App is used and improve it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash reports, stack traces, and device/app information to help us find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config — fetches configuration values to adjust App behavior without requiring an update; this does not involve your birth details or chat content.

These services are provided by Google. See Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

2.7 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and supported by ads shown through the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK, including app-open, interstitial, and native ad formats. AdMob and its partner ad networks may collect an advertising identifier, device information, and app-interaction data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's policies. See Google's Partner Policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2.8 Character images (content delivery network)

Some in-app illustrations/character images are loaded from a content delivery network (CDN) operated by our infrastructure provider. Loading these images may expose your device's IP address and basic request information to that CDN in the ordinary course of delivering the images; no birth details or chat content are included in these requests.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to: calculate and personalise your horoscope/kundli and AI chat responses, remember your data across sessions via your recovery code, send optional daily horoscope notifications, diagnose and fix crashes, understand aggregate usage to improve the App, and serve and measure advertising.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. The only parties that may receive data, and only the categories described above, are:

  • Our AI service provider (dev.bbltech.org) — receives your chat questions and birth details to generate astrology readings.
  • Google (Firebase) — analytics, crash diagnostics, remote configuration.
  • Google (AdMob) and its mediation/ad-network partners — advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads.
  • Our content delivery network (CDN) provider — basic request data needed to deliver in-app images.

5. Data Retention and Your Choices

  • Birth details, chat history, and settings are stored on your device and, so they can be restored via your recovery code, on our AI service provider's server. They remain until you request deletion (see our Data Deletion page) or until you uninstall the App without having saved/restored your recovery code, which abandons the on-device copy.
  • Analytics/crash data held by Firebase is retained per Google's standard retention periods.
  • Advertising identifier data held by AdMob/ad partners is retained and reset according to Google's policies; you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Rajwaad Jyotish is intended for a general adult audience (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at minhhang68930@gmail.com and we will address it.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete data associated with you, or to object to certain processing. Contact us at minhhang68930@gmail.com to exercise these rights, or see our Data Deletion page for a step-by-step deletion request process.

8. Security

We rely on Android's app sandboxing to keep your on-device data private to the App, encrypted connections when the App communicates with our AI service and Google's services, and Google's security practices for Firebase and AdMob. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9. Astrology Disclaimer

Horoscopes, kundli readings, and AI chat responses provided by the App are for entertainment purposes only. They are not medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice. See our Terms of Use for the full disclaimer.
