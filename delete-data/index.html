Data Deletion

Rajwaad Jyotish · Effective date: September 14, 2026

Rajwaad Jyotish does not use an email/password sign-up system — there is no username or password tied to your identity. Instead, the App identifies your installation with an anonymous, device-generated recovery code, which you can save and later use to restore your birth details, chat history, and settings on another device. This page explains how to delete your data, whether you've saved a recovery code or not.

1. Request deletion of your data (recommended — works whether or not you have your recovery code)

  1. Email minhhang68930@gmail.com with the subject line "Data deletion request — Rajwaad Jyotish".
  2. If you have it, include your recovery code so we can locate your data quickly. If you don't have it, tell us the approximate date you started using the App and, if you remember it, the name/date of birth you entered — this helps us locate the right record.
  3. We will delete your birth details, chat history, and recovery-code record from our AI service provider's server, and confirm by email, within 30 days.

2. Remove your data from this device right now

You can also remove the copy of your data stored locally on your device immediately, in either of two ways:

Option A — Clear the App's data (keeps the App installed)
  1. Open your device's Settings.
  2. Go to Apps (or Apps & notifications).
  3. Select Rajwaad Jyotish.
  4. Tap Storage & cache.
  5. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data).

This immediately erases your locally stored birth details, chat history, and settings from this device. If you have not saved your recovery code beforehand, this data cannot later be restored on this or any other device.

Option B — Uninstall the App
  1. Press and hold the Rajwaad Jyotish app icon.
  2. Tap Uninstall (or drag to the Uninstall option).
  3. Confirm.

Uninstalling removes the App and its locally stored data from your device immediately. As with Option A, if you have not saved your recovery code, this data cannot later be restored.

Clearing local data or uninstalling the App removes it from your device right away, but does not by itself delete the copy associated with your recovery code on our AI service provider's server — use the email request in Section 1 to have that copy deleted as well.
